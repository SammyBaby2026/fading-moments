## 四、Batteries 页面方向

### Hero
**Headline:** Custom Lithium Battery Packs for Industrial Applications  
**Subheadline:** Application-specific lithium battery systems with BMS integration, optional communication and tailored mechanical design.

### Focus Areas
- **Custom lithium battery packs** — Not utility-scale storage, but industrial equipment batteries
- **LiFePO4-based systems** — Stable chemistry for industrial duty
- **BMS integration** — Cell monitoring, balancing, protection
- **CAN / Bluetooth optional** — Communication interfaces for system integration
- **Custom dimensions / enclosure** — Tailored to installation constraints
- **Industrial integration** — Built for equipment, not grid services

### Product Types
1. **Standard lithium packs** — Configurable standard designs for common voltages/capacities
2. **Application-specific designs** — Tailored to mechanical, thermal, electrical requirements
3. **OEM battery systems** — White-label or co-developed battery solutions

### Key Message
This is not public energy storage. This is custom battery engineering for industrial equipment, vehicles and mobile systems.
